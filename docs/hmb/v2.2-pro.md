# Hamrah v2.2 Pro

Date: 2026-10-28. Local implementation built on the existing v2.1 changes.

## What changed

The site now carries one editorial design through its public pages and operational screens. Hamrah's paper, deep green and lime remain primary; the selective amber capabilities and sign-in scenes from v2.1 remain. The taste attachments are unchanged.

Public pages share a breadcrumb/context strip, an original layered worksheet illustration, a more deliberate type scale, consistent card and reading surfaces, and a green closing action. Course summaries, syllabuses, journal tabs, article sources, policy panels and empty states use the same visual grammar. Details: [public theme](v2.2-subpages.md).

Applications, receipt states, checkout and member/coach/admin/editor workspaces received scoped presentation refinements. Forms preserve field labels, validation and submission behavior. Private data and controls are not animated on scroll. Details: [workspace theme](v2.2-workspaces.md).

## Homepage motion

- The header stays available while scrolling, with a subtle surface/shadow change and a thin reading-progress indicator.
- The hero studio and selected decorative layers gain modest scroll depth on desktop. Effects reset on tablet/mobile.
- A new four-step story connects understanding, practice, recording and feedback. A sticky worksheet changes with the reader's position; ordinary anchor links also navigate between steps.
- Position tracking uses a passive scroll listener and one requestAnimationFrame, with state changes only when the active step changes. It handles fast wheel/PageDown jumps and direct anchors; there is no scroll capture or snapping.
- Section reveals and card staggers use one-shot intersection observation. Hover effects remain available after entrance animations. Keyboard focus bypasses reveal delays.
- Short desktop viewports use a compact worksheet, and very short screens use a static layout. Mobile shows all four readable step cards without the pinned visual.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable entrances, translation and sticky story effects. Initial HTML contains all narrative content and destinations.

## Integration review

The orchestrator coordinated public-theme and workspace specialists, plus an independent motion source review. The review caught observer activation margins, short-viewport fit, stale tablet offsets and focus visibility. Browser testing also exposed a skipped-stage case during fast scrolling; the final story uses deterministic position tracking. Journal tabs now support Home/End as well as RTL arrow navigation. Sticky side panels account for the shared header.
