# HMB product brief

Snapshot: 2026-10-21. Authority: owner-supplied `master-brief.txt`; the user requested a new project. This is a local source implementation and reviewable preview, not a launch at hamrah.me. No existing HMB repository, journal, approved assets or external services were supplied in this workspace. See [evidence](evidence.md) and [decisions](decisions.md).

## Working proposition

HMB helps traders turn knowledge into repeatable practice through structured learning, a record of decisions and human feedback. This is the brief's proposed positioning, not a validated outcome claim or an extracted brand-book statement. Audiences are starting learners, active traders seeking a practice routine, and experienced traders seeking focused review. These are working hypotheses, not researched segments.

| Stage | Visitor need | Mechanism | Visible artifact | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 Understand | Where should I start? | Need/experience assessment | Application and selected need | `/apply`, `/approach` |
| V2 Learn | What should I learn? | Structured concepts and educational notes | Proposed syllabus, short exercise | `/programs`, `/insights` |
| V3 Practise | How do I use it? | Deliberate practice | Preparation checklist | `/programs/practice` |
| V4 Record | What actually happened? | Decision journal | Plan, reasoning, execution, reflection | `/journal`, `/member` |
| V5 Review | What should I change? | Assigned human coach | Observation and suggested exercise | `/coaching`, `/coach` |
| V6 Improve | What comes next? | Follow-up action and another review | Acknowledged feedback and next action | `/member` |

The implementation supports this operating chain: discovery → application → staff review → manually provisioned/assigned membership → private journal → coach feedback → member action. Staffing, acceptance capacity, response time, teaching delivery and commercial terms remain owner decisions. The database and queues are implemented; they are not evidence of a staffed HMB service.

## Actual homepage storyboard

| Scene | Implemented content | Action |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Recognition | Persian opening, educational journal/feedback artifacts, clear absence of profit promises | `/apply`, `/approach` |
| H2 Gap | Preparation/execution/review mismatches in three concise situations | Read into method |
| H3 Route | Four interactive educational stages: learn, practise, record, feedback | `/approach` |
| H4 Practice | Three-question exercise worksheet | `/programs` |
| H5 Journal | Fictional practice entry showing plan and actual action | `/journal` |
| H6 Human feedback | Fictional observation attached to that entry | `/coaching` |
| H7 Trust | Process, respect for the decision-maker, and boundaries; no invented team profiles | `/about`, `/events` |
| H8 Next step | Selector for foundations, practice or coaching | Canonical selected offer page |

Three educational article links, an accessible FAQ and a final application action complete the homepage. All demonstration data is synthetic, with labels. Supporting pages contain substantive Persian content. The design avoids market tickers, performance graphs, fabricated reviews and a star logo.

## Scope and limits

P0: public routes, applications and staff queue, editorial UI, basic safe measurement, contact/policy explanations. P1: isolated member core, assigned-coach review, revisions, export and deletion requests. P2 providers, payments, Telegram synchronization, calendar bookings, advanced analytics, LMS and partner ecosystem are deferred. No checkout, notifications, event seat reservation, actual course delivery or production deployment is claimed.
