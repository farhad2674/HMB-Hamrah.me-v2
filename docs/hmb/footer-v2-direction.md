# Footer artwork reassessment

Date: 2026-10-28.

The previous marble-hands image followed the supplied visual reference too literally. Marble suggested permanence and cultural prestige, while the open book suggested generic education. Neither showed what distinguishes Hamrah: a written plan, a record of execution, and a coach's observation that informs the next practice. The owner's feedback rejected that metaphor.

The replacement direction is a decision-review workspace with three connected product artifacts. In RTL reading order: a pre-decision checklist and risk boundary; a comparison of planned action with recorded execution; and a feedback annotation that identifies the next practice. Green/lime light belongs to the brand, rather than to a borrowed sculpture aesthetic.

The illustration deliberately avoids hands, marble, books, performance charts, income symbols, trophies and fabricated interface metrics. Three small HTML labels make the process legible without relying on text baked into an image. The footer links and decorative Hamrah wordmark remain.

The replacement is generated with the built-in image_gen tool with transparent_background=true. Exact prompt: footer-v2-prompt.json. The previous source asset remains on disk as an earlier iteration and is no longer referenced by the footer.
