# Hamrah v2.1 Pro — implemented direction

Date: 2026-10-28. Local review build; no production deployment performed.

## Message and art direction

The central idea is «هر تصمیم، پشتوانه‌ای می‌خواهد.» The homepage now introduces Hamrah through the quality of a decision, demonstrates a written plan and review, then connects the visitor to an appropriate learning route. Persian copy names the activity and destination instead of repeating generic promises. Copy coverage and source research are documented in [v2.1-copy.md](v2.1-copy.md).

The owner's final colour direction takes priority over the references: Hamrah's warm paper #f8f7f3, deep green #172b2b and lime #d2f36b lead the site. Light editorial surfaces alternate with a dark green decision studio and closing section. Amber is a selective accent in the capabilities and member sign-in scenes. The supplied attachment files are unchanged.

| Reference | Applied idea | Hamrah adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipcraft CTA and footer | Spacious closing statement, polished action, oversized closing wordmark | Green/lime invitation and four-part footer; original wordmark treatment; no unrelated app editor, newsletter or invented social accounts |
| Capabilities — amber Clari | Fine borders, holographic objects, restrained amber light | Three original SVG learning/journal/review objects in a dark capabilities scene |
| Project Estimation Calculator | Two-column input/result interaction | Need-based route selector with actual course/coaching destinations; no fabricated course prices or calculation |
| Keylight signin | Split composition, a focused glass form, staged entrance | Actual email/password member sign-in with password visibility, API errors and role redirects preserved |

All artwork is original CSS/SVG and educational sample content. No external image requests, testimonials, performance charts or generated team identities were added. Reading pages retain comfortable light surfaces. The hero and login enter once and settle; scroll reveals do not trap scrolling. Motion preferences are respected.

## Reference research

Reviewed Hosseini Finance's homepage, online course listing, in-person offering, about, contact and blog pages, plus the ICT, price action, gold/indices, crypto, beginner forex and trading-plan course detail pages. The useful patterns were specific learning topics, audience/fit explanations, explicit syllabus sections and distinct contact purposes. Hamrah copy is original. Reference business statistics, staff claims, financial promises, prices and delivery commitments were not transferred. This is qualitative research across relevant page types, not an exhaustive crawl of the blog archive.

Key source pages: [home](https://hoseinifinance.com/), [online courses](https://hoseinifinance.com/courses/), [in-person programme](https://hoseinifinance.com/elite/), [price action](https://hoseinifinance.com/course/337137/), [beginner forex](https://hoseinifinance.com/course/506207/), [trading plan](https://hoseinifinance.com/course/519872/).
