# v2.1 editorial direction and coverage

Date: 2026-10-28. This pass accompanies the v2.1 art direction; it is a local reviewable implementation.

## Voice and message

Hamrah connects forex education to a written plan, recorded execution and review. Public copy addresses the reader respectfully, uses concrete verbs, and makes the activity and next action clear. Headlines may express an idea; the adjacent description names the actual educational product or workflow. Course detail and legal descriptions retain the conditions required to make an informed decision.

We removed repeated slogans about a “clear next step,” dense search-oriented introductory paragraphs, and mascot captions from the public subpages. Sample journals remain explicitly fictional. Course prices, dates, instructors, capacity, response times and commercial terms are not invented.

## Reference research

The owner supplied https://hoseinifinance.com as a copy reference. The orchestrator reviewed the main course and homepage material; this editorial pass also reviewed:

- https://hoseinifinance.com/about-us/ — a distinct institutional introduction and sections that explain the teaching approach.
- https://hoseinifinance.com/contact-us/ — separate actions for educational enquiries and account/support needs.
- https://hoseinifinance.com/blog/ — concrete educational topics and descriptive article links.

The useful patterns are specificity, purposeful navigation and separating visitor needs. The wording here is original Hamrah copy. No audience statistics, instructor histories, founder details, staffing claims, contact information or service promises from the reference were transferred. This was qualitative copy research, not an exhaustive crawl, keyword-volume study or independent verification of that site's claims.

The four supplied taste files inform visual composition and interaction. Their fictional product names, business promises, testimonials, authentication methods and build instructions do not define Hamrah's product scope.

## Metadata and existing articles

Public page metadata was reviewed against its existing route. Descriptive titles such as “کوچینگ ترید فارکس و بازخورد معاملاتی” remain appropriate even when the visible headline is more editorial. Course metadata continues to describe syllabus and registration conditions; CMS SEO overrides remain intact. Canonicals, robots, route names and publication policy were preserved.

Existing article titles, summaries and image/figure headings were inspected in `content/blog/`. They are topic-specific educational headings, with sources and exercises, and were preserved rather than replaced with sales slogans. The article route's surrounding labels were refined. Legal bodies were preserved because the underlying service scope, data behavior and commercial readiness did not change; this pass is not a legal or financial review.
