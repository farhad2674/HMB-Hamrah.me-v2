# v2.2 public subpage theme

Date: 2026-10-28. Builds on the existing v2.1 copy and brand decisions.

## Design

The public pages now share an editorial entrance: breadcrumb/context strip, a restrained display heading, native Persian description, original layered worksheet/orbital artwork and a quiet closing rule. Journal art uses a deep green worksheet; article and policy variants reduce decoration to protect reading space. Paper #f8f7f3, deep green #172b2b and lime #d2f36b remain the main palette.

Public roots use `.subpage-shell` so their new card, reading, journal and course treatments remain separate from the homepage, sign-in and private workspace surfaces. `src/app/subpages.css` is imported by the orchestrator after the brand sheet. Sections and selected grids expose `data-reveal` / `data-stagger` to the shared motion system.

Dynamic course, event, article and service route templates retain their publication/data contracts. Legal bodies, course availability, CTA destinations and public sample disclosures are unchanged. No external media, dependencies or business claims were added.
